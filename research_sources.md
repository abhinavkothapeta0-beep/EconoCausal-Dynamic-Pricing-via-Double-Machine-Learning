# EconoCausal: Research Sources and References

## 1. Purpose

This document maintains the original research publications, dataset sources and technical references used in the EconoCausal project.

## 2. Primary Research Publication

**Title:** A rich dataset of hourly residential electricity consumption data and survey answers from the iFlex dynamic pricing experiment

**Authors:** Matthias Hofmann and Turid Siebenbrunner

**Publication Year:** 2023

**Journal:** Data in Brief

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2023.109571

**Purpose:**
To understand the experiment design, research background, participant information and electricity pricing methodology.

## 3. Primary Dataset

**Dataset Name:** iFlex Dynamic Pricing Experiment Dataset

**Platform:** Zenodo

**Dataset URL:** https://zenodo.org/records/8248802

**Purpose:**
The dataset is the planned primary source for household electricity consumption and experimental electricity price information.

**Download Status:** Not downloaded yet.
